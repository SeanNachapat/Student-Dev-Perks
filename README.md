## ✅ How to Verify Your Student Status

Most services will ask you to prove enrollment through one of these methods:

| Method | Details | Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 📧 **School Email** | Sign up or verify with your `.edu` / `.ac.uk` / institutional email. | Fastest method — works with most services. |
| 🪪 **Student ID Upload** | Upload a photo of your student ID showing your name and expiry date. | Make sure the current term or expiry date is visible. |
| 📄 **Enrollment Letter / Transcript** | Upload an official document from your registrar's office. | Used as a fallback if email verification isn't available. |

> [!TIP]
> **Start with the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)** — verifying once there unlocks dozens of the benefits listed above automatically.
